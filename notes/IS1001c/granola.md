## Previous Meeting Decisions (Recap)
- Simple interface agreed
- Wheel for channel control, previous channel button
- Channel digit buttons protected by plastic cover
- Volume and power buttons included
- Lighting feature for easy findability
- No LCD or voice features (cost and speed rationale)

## Component Decisions
- Standard battery confirmed
- Chip-on-print: specific chip identified, no issues
- Wheel sensor retained despite cost
  - ~50x more expensive than a push button
  - Kept for innovation and ease-of-use advantage over competitors
  - Total BOM must stay under €12
- Plastic cover for channel buttons reconsidered
  - Concern: covers break, harder to use
  - Alternative: recess or offset channel buttons slightly instead
- Backlit buttons confirmed
  - Activation trigger debated: motion sensor vs. power button press
  - Battery consumption flagged as concern for always-on lighting

## Voice Recognition Update
- Email from manufacturing division: voice recognition chip already developed internally
- Currently used to record answers to questions; could potentially recognize commands
- Decision deferred: revisit after presentations

## Design Direction: Banana Form Factor
- Trend research from Paris and Milan: fruits/vegetables theme, spongy materials
- Key priorities ranked: fancy look and feel > technological innovation > ease of use
- Banana shape selected
  - Yellow: matches company color scheme, easy to locate
  - Ergonomic curve fits hand naturally
  - Wheel on top, buttons on the side
- Construction: hard internal structure with spongy outer cover
  - Buttons and wheel must remain operable through the cover
- Left/right hand symmetry considered
  - Design deemed workable for both; lighted buttons help orient the correct side
