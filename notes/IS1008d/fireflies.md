## Notes
### Prototype Design and Features
The team finalized an ergonomic, customizable wooden remote with a flip panel LCD display option.
- Prototype presentation highlighted core functions including on/off, volume, channel, menu, and voice control integrated on a flip panel with a yellow LCD screen, which can be removed if too costly (02:47)
  - Buttons are color-coded for ease: yellow for power, red for volume, green for channels, blue for numeric keypad
  - Customizable front and back panels allow users to personalize their remote
  - The microphone placement remains undecided but likely on the side to allow voice activation even when closed
  - Weight and button shapes were noted for refinement, balancing heft for control without being too heavy
- Discussion emphasized voice control usability and design trade-offs with voice activation needing to work even when the flip is closed (05:50)
  - The side placement of the microphone was favored to avoid forcing users to open the panel for voice commands
  - Weight considerations stemmed from user preference for a substantial feel to enhance perceived control
  - The prototype material (Plasticine) limited exact weight modeling but indicated design intent for a solid feel

### Cost Analysis and Financial Viability
The projected production cost is below budget, allowing flexibility for marketing and customization expenses.
- Initial unit cost estimated at €9.95, under the €12.50 budget, excluding the LCD display cost (07:13)
  - Adding the LCD would raise cost by €2-3, pushing unit cost closer to €12.50, matching company targets
  - Production cost includes materials and labor, with labor for the wooden case estimated at about €1
  - Negotiations with vendors planned to reduce costs further to allocate more budget for marketing and promotions
- Profitability depends on capturing significant market share and price targets (12:46)
  - Market capacity approximated at 8-9 million units annually; company aims for 2 million units, requiring 25% market share in year one
  - Target retail price set at €50 per unit, with production cost not exceeding €25 to achieve €50 million profit goal
  - Additional costs like advertising and distribution are not included, indicating tight margins
  - Bulk production may reduce circuit board cost but customization keeps case cost fixed

### Market Strategy and Sales Channels
The team explored alternative sales channels and timing to maximize market adoption and profitability.
- Online sales and drop shipping proposed to reduce storage and retail distribution costs (14:11)
  - Selling via platforms like Amazon or eBay could showcase customization options and increase customer engagement
  - Online visualization tools (rotating images, videos) suggested to help buyers choose custom designs remotely
  - This approach aligns with growing consumer comfort buying tactile products online despite lack of physical feel
- Launch timing tied to major events to boost sales impact (20:06)
  - Launch synchronization with major sports events like the World Cup or popular movie releases proposed to leverage high viewership
  - Collaboration with media companies such as Sony Pictures considered for co-marketing opportunities
  - Event-driven launches allow time for user testing and feedback incorporation before broad market introduction

### Product Evaluation and Launch Planning
The team agreed on a fixed design for initial launch with plans for iterative improvement based on user feedback.
- No redesign time allocated before launch; initial model to go directly to market (18:48)
  - Feedback from early users to inform second-generation product development post-launch
  - Acknowledgement that lacking redesign before launch risks failure if initial product misses key user needs
  - Balancing price, feature set, and market readiness was a critical consideration in fixing the design
- Prototype production and market testing timeline set at 2-4 weeks (17:11)
  - Prototypes to be developed externally and tested in real market environments
  - Quick turnaround intended to gather user insights without delaying launch
  - Final production to begin only after product evaluation confirms readiness
