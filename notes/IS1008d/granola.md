## Prototype Design
- Ergonomic wooden remote with customizable stain/color finish
- Flip panel with yellow LCD display
- Button layout:
  - Yellow: on/off (large, prominent)
  - Red triangles: volume up/down
  - Green: channel change
  - Dark blue: numeric pad
  - Right side: TV menu access
  - Left side: voice recognition toggle
- Customizable backing panel (front and back)
- Microphone placement unresolved: under flip ruled out; sides proposed as best option
  - Key argument: voice should work with remote closed
- Weight intentional: users prefer something substantial in hand
- Speaker/mic placement depends on final circuit board layout

## Budget and Production Cost
- Target budget: €12.50 per unit
- Current production cost: €9.95 (under budget)
- LCD display adds ~€2 to €3 per unit, bringing total to ~€12 to €12.50
- Customization limits bulk cost savings on the case; circuit board produced at scale
- Plan to negotiate with vendors to reduce production cost further

## Market Strategy and Financials
- Market size: 8 to 9 million units/year (independent study + internal estimate aligned)
- Company target: 2 million units in year one = 25% market share
- Required retail price: €50; max production cost: €25 per unit
- Required profit per unit: €25 to hit €50M total profit target
- Online-first sales channel proposed:
  - Drop-ship direct to customer (no retail storage)
  - Sell via Amazon or eBay
  - Web configurator lets buyers visualize customization options (images, 3D rotation)
  - Could unlock sales beyond 2M units and reduce unit cost

## Launch Timing and User Testing
- Prototype evaluation: 2 to 4 weeks (external facility, not in-house)
- Concern raised: no redesign window built into timeline if user testing surfaces issues
- Strategy proposed: sync launch with a major sports event (e.g., World Cup)
  - Gives more time for proper user testing
  - Natural advertising opportunity for a voice-controlled remote
- Partnership idea: work with studios (e.g., Sony Pictures) on DVD release tie-ins
- Decision: proceed with current model for market introduction; gather feedback for second generation
- Historical note raised: products launched without sufficient user testing often flop, then succeed on relaunch (disposable diapers cited as example)
