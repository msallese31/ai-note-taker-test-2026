## Costs against the market target
- The base unit estimate came in under budget, but the LCD and the company's sales and profit goals put feasibility in doubt.
- The initial estimate was €9.95 against a €12.50 budget. [07:26]
  - The estimate included energy sources, a chip, sensors, a speaker, wood, color, and buttons.
  - The LCD price was not included; adding it could cost about €2–€3 per remote.
- The cost estimate excluded promotion and transportation. [09:38]
  - The company target was €50 million profit.
  - Christine estimated the case could cost €1 for material and labor.
- The market study estimated annual demand at eight million units, while the internal estimate was eight to nine million. [12:23]
  - Selling two million units in year one would require 25% of the market.
  - At €50 per unit, production cost could not exceed €25 to leave €25 profit per unit.
  - The group noted additional costs for transport, promotion, and labor.
- Christine proposed online ordering and direct shipment as an alternative sales channel. [14:16]
  - Online sales could avoid storage and transport to retail points, though delivery and labor would remain.
  - The group suggested Amazon or eBay and product customization images or rotation views.
  - The marketing participant said an online channel might support sales above two million units and lower unit costs.
