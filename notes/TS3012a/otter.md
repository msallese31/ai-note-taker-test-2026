## Summary
The team met for the first time to kick off TS3012a, a project dedicated to designing an original, trendy, and user-friendly television remote control.

### Project Overview
Markoff introduced the team and the primary objective of creating a remote control inspired by current fashion, electronics, and interior design trends. The project will proceed through three distinct design phases, beginning with functional design.

### Design Requirements and Financials
- The product must be distinct from competitors while remaining intuitive for users.
- The target selling price is 25 euros.
- The approximate production cost is 12.50 euros.

### Team Roles
Markoff serves as the Project Manager and will oversee the agenda and documentation via a central projects folder.

### Expertise and Contributions
- Dirk is the marketing expert responsible for analyzing user needs and market trends.
- Speaker 4 acts as the User Interface Designer focusing on technical design from a user perspective.
- Savier is the Industrial Designer tasked with determining the internal apparatus components, materials, and aesthetics.

### Initial Discussions
The team used a Smart Board/Doodling Board to conduct an icebreaker activity involving drawing animals to share personal values like freedom (Speaker 4) or efficiency (Savier).

### Open Design Questions
- Should the device be designed as a multi-remote control or limited strictly to television functions?
- How can the design accommodate users with disabilities to ensure accessibility?
- What specific lightweight materials will best suit the industrial design requirements?
