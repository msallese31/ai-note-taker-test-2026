## Project Overview
- Designing a new TV remote control
- Design goals: original, trendy, user-friendly
- Positioning: “fashion electronics,” inspired by trends in clothes and interior design
- Selling price: €25; production price: ~€12.50

## Team Introductions
- Mark Knopf: project manager
- Dirk Mengveld: marketing expert, focused on user needs and new product direction
- Xavier Jökens: industrial designer, tasked with answering:
  1. What happens inside the apparatus
  2. What is it made of
  3. What should it look like
- UI designer also present (name not captured)

## Three-Phase Design Process
- Phase 1: Functional design (current phase)
- Each team member completes individual work, then shares findings at the next meeting
- Minutes and materials stored in the shared projects folder
- Next meeting in 30 minutes; personal assignments to be distributed via personal coach

## Early Design Considerations
- Scope confirmed: TV remote only, not a multi-device controller
- Intuitive design: stick to what users are familiar with, rational changes only
- Accessibility: consider users with disabilities as a potential design requirement
- Material: should be lightweight
- Budget is tight at €12.50 production cost

## Whiteboard Session
